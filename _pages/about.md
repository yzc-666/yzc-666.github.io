I am a PhD student at the [School of Computing and Data Science](https://www.cds.hku.hk/), The University of Hong Kong, advised by [Prof. Difan Zou](https://difanzou.github.io/). I work on **post-training** and **interpretability** of large language models.

#### Research interests

- **Post-training**: methods such as on-policy distillation and reinforcement learning that improve large language models after pretraining.
- **Interpretability**: mechanistic interpretability of large language models, especially what they learn during post-training.
