Research Interests

What changes inside language models during post-training?

Mechanistic Interpretability

I want to understand how internal representations and circuits give rise to model behavior, and how those mechanisms change across training checkpoints.

Post-Training

I am interested in how on-policy distillation and reinforcement learning shape the capabilities and reasoning behavior of pretrained language models.
