I am a PhD student at the [School of Computing and Data Science](https://www.cds.hku.hk/), The University of Hong Kong, advised by [Prof. Difan Zou](https://difanzou.github.io/). I work on **AI4AI**, **autoresearch**, and **large language models**, and I am also interested in interdisciplinary research that connects AI with other scientific fields.

My recent work studies on-policy distillation, reinforcement learning for diffusion language models, and fast, physics-consistent sampling for diffusion and flow models.
