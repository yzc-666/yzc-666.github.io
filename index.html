Research Interests

Broadly, I want to understand why learning algorithms work. Three threads I keep coming back to:

01

Generalization Theory

Why do overparameterized models generalize when classical bounds say they should not? Implicit bias, margin theory, and what the right complexity measure actually is.

  • PAC-Bayes
  • implicit bias
  • benign overfitting
02

Optimization Dynamics

Non-convex landscapes, the trajectory gradient methods actually take, and convergence guarantees that survive contact with practice.

  • SGD
  • non-convex
  • convergence rates
03

Fundamental Limits

Lower bounds, minimax rates, and information-theoretic barriers — the results that tell you when to stop looking for a better algorithm.

  • minimax
  • lower bounds
  • information theory
