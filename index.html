I am an incoming PhD student at the School of Computing and Data Science, HKU, starting in Fall 2026. My research centres on large language models and machine learning more broadly.

I am drawn to the questions that sit underneath the benchmarks: how these models actually acquire their capabilities, where those capabilities break down, and what we can say about them with some rigour rather than by anecdote.

I am always glad to hear from people working on similar problems — whether that is a collaboration, a question about something I have written, or a paper you think I should read.
