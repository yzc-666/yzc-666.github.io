I am a PhD student at the [School of Computing and Data Science](https://www.cds.hku.hk/), The University of Hong Kong, advised by [Prof. Difan Zou](https://difanzou.github.io/). I work on **AI4AI**, **autoresearch**, and **large language models**, and I am also interested in interdisciplinary research that connects AI with other scientific fields.

## Research Interests

- **AI4AI**: using AI to improve how AI itself is built, from training recipes and data to evaluation.
- **Autoresearch**: agents that carry out the research loop end to end, from forming hypotheses to designing experiments and analyzing results.
- **Large Language Models**: post-training methods such as on-policy distillation and reinforcement learning, and mechanistic interpretability of what models learn along the way.
- **Interdisciplinary AI**: AI for science, especially generative models that respect physical laws when inferring physical fields from sparse observations.
